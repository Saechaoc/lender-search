# Plan 03-05: VA Pamphlet 26-7 derog matrix — full event-type parity + golden snapshot

## Objective

Hand-author the VA Pamphlet 26-7 Chapter 4 Topic 7 derog waiting-period matrix as a TS fixture. Same structural pattern as Plans 03-02/03/04 with VA-specific timings. MULTIPLE_BK is SKIPPED per Open Question 5 (VA, like FHA, defers to standard EC).

Per RESEARCH §"Per-agency content matrix" §VA Pamphlet 26-7:
- **BK7:** 24m base, 12m EC, anchor=DISCHARGE
- **BK13_DISCHARGED:** 24m base, anchor=DISCHARGE (12m if 12 months pay-out elapsed with trustee approval — Phase 3 ships baseline post-discharge variant)
- **BK13_DISMISSED:** 24m base, 12m EC, anchor=DISMISSAL
- **MULTIPLE_BK:** SKIPPED per Open Question 5
- **FORECLOSURE:** 24m base, 12m EC, anchor=COMPLETION (VA shorter than FHA's 36m)
- **DEED_IN_LIEU:** 24m base, 12m EC, anchor=COMPLETION
- **SHORT_SALE:** 24m base, 12m EC, anchor=SALE_CONFIRMATION
- **MORTGAGE_CHARGE_OFF:** 24m base, 12m EC, anchor=CHARGE_OFF_DATE

Per Pitfall 1.7 (research): VA itself imposes no waiting after FC for borrowers using NEW VA entitlement — the 24m baseline reflects typical lender overlay. Phase 3 ships the typical baseline; second-tier-entitlement modeling is deferred to Phase 4+ evaluator nuance.

URL-only citations under SYSTEM tenant per D-07. Source: https://www.benefits.va.gov/warms/docs/admin26/pamphlet/pam26_7/ch04.pdf
